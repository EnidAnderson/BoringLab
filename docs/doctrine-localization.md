# LAB A2.0 — doctrine-localization: existence, universal property, inversion as data

Companion note to `docs/port-typing.md`, covering
`BoringLab/Doctrine/Localization.lean` (built on `BoringLab/Doctrine/Doctrine.lean`).
Issue: `EnidAnderson/BoringLab#3`, scope contract part 2 ("first theorems: the
localization exists; declared-move inversion is data, not a global axiom").

## What landed

All in `BoringLab/Doctrine/Localization.lean`, every declaration a one-line
term-mode proof, `-- Reference:` above each, no `axiom`, no `noncomputable`
declaration:

- `Doctrine.declared_move_wIso` — a declared move `p ⇄ q` gives
  `Nonempty (Iso (Q.obj X) (Q.obj Y))`, witnessed by
  `Localization.Construction.wIso p (moveClass_left …)`. Inversion as data.
- `Doctrine.lift_exists` — universal property, existence:
  `∃ L : d.moveClass.Localization ⥤ E, d.moveClass.Q ⋙ L = F`, witnessed by
  `Localization.Construction.lift F hF` with `Construction.fac`.
- `Doctrine.lift_unique` — universal property, uniqueness, via
  `Localization.Construction.uniq`.
- `misereQuotient_related_iso` — the falsifiable shadow, now *derived*:
  `r X Y → Nonempty (Iso ((misereQuotient r).moveClass.Q.obj X)
  (… .obj Y))`. The generator path of an `r`-related pair is a declared move
  (`misereQuotient_move_self`), hence inverted.

## Prop-shadow decision (choice-taint discipline)

At pin `v4.35.0-rc3`, `Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Localization/Construction.lean`
sits in a `noncomputable section` (line 45): `MorphismProperty.Localization`,
`MorphismProperty.Q`, `wIso`/`wInv`, `Construction.lift` are choice-tainted.
House rule honored: these names appear **only inside `theorem` (Prop)
statements**, never in a `def`. Consequence: the two isomorphism-producing
theorems are stated as `Nonempty (Iso …)` (a Prop) rather than returning the
`Iso` itself, which would force a `noncomputable def`. The explicit inverse
laws remain available as the Prop-valued `Doctrine.declared_move_inversion`
(`∃ inv, Q.map p ≫ inv = 𝟙 _ ∧ inv ≫ Q.map p = 𝟙 _`, witnessed by `wInv`).

## Repair note (universe constraint)

The recovered `Doctrine.lean` failed to build at
`misereQuotient_generator_inverted` with
`stuck at solving universe constraint max ?u.24 ?u.25 =?= u + 1`
(`Doctrine.{max ?u.24 ?u.25}` vs `Doctrine.{u+1}`): applying
`Doctrine.declared_move_inverted` (whose statement carries the pin's
`max u₁ u₂` from `MorphismProperty.Q`'s functor type) to
`misereQuotient r : Doctrine.{u+1}` leaves the second universe metavariable
unsolvable. Fix: the proof now applies `MorphismProperty.Q_inverts` directly
to `moveClass_left (misereQuotient_move_self …)` (probe `probeD5`), and the
four `Q`-mentioning theorems take `(d : Doctrine.{u})` explicitly, which also
clears the `linter.checkUnivs` warnings. `lake build BoringLab.Doctrine.*`
is now warning- and error-clean.
